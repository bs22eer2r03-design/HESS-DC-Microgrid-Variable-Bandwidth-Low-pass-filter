## Simulation file Design Specifications:
Simulation parameter Details : The system consists of a battery unit rated at 48 V, 28 Ah, a supercapacitor (SC) unit rated at 48 V with a capacitance of 19.3 F, and a PV panel with a voltage of 60 V and a current of 15.8 A and apply P and O algoritm or any MPPT algorithm. Two bi-directional converters interface the storage units, each with an inductance of 1.5 mH (L_sc = L_bat = 1.5 mH) and a capacitance of 440 µF (C_bat = C_sc = 440 µF). The load voltage is 96 V, the converters operate at a switching frequency of 10 kHz, and the rated output power of the system is 1 kW. Based on this analysis, a PI controller is designed, and the corresponding Bode plots are ploted to assess the closed-loop system stability is given in matlab folder codes.
